CRITICAL BOARDING RULE

Direction Matters: NH-5 Highway Boarding Sides at Waknaghat

⚠️ Always confirm direction with conductor
▲ UPHILL SIDE (Mountain Side)
Towards: Shimla, Kufri, Narkanda, Theog, Rampur, Kinnaur
Cross to the mountain side (passenger shelter & shops). All buses climbing up to Shimla stop here.
Look for: SHIMLA / ISBT TUTIKANDI
signpost WAKNAGHAT JUNCTION
JUIT Link Road connects here (~2.5 km from campus gate)
▼ DOWNHILL SIDE (Valley Side)
Towards: Kandaghat, Solan, Dharampur, Kasauli, Kalka, Chandigarh, Delhi
Stand on the valley side (facing south down the hill). Buses heading towards Solan, Kalka, and Chandigarh pull over here.
Look for: SOLAN / CHANDIGARH 43 / DELHI

Understanding Himachal Buses & Travel Terms

Essential practical knowledge for first-time student travelers in Himachal Pradesh.

HRTC
Himachal Road Transport Corporation
The state-owned public bus service. Safe hill drivers, extensive network reaching every remote valley. Green/blue livery.
Ordinary Bus
Regular 3x2 Seater Hill Bus
Most common and affordable service. Frequent departures from Waknaghat. Buy ticket directly from the conductor inside the bus.
Deluxe / Semi-Deluxe
2x2 Comfortable Coach
Higher comfort seating, limited stops, faster transit on long-distance runs (e.g. Shimla–Chandigarh, Shimla–Manali).
Volvo / Scania AC
Premium Highway Cruiser
Air-conditioned multi-axle luxury coaches running between Shimla, Chandigarh, and Delhi. Advance booking available on hrtchp.com.
ISBT
Inter-State Bus Terminal
Major regional bus stations with designated numbered departure bays, ticket counters, waiting halls, and cloakrooms.
